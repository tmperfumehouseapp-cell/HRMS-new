Tidy Teach questions

Remove repeated and no-longer-needed questions from the Teach board

What this does

It only shortens the list of questions the Teach board still asks. What the bot already learned (concepts, rules, limits and every answer you gave) is not touched. It removes questions that repeat another question, keeps at most 3 questions in a topic whose concept is already understood (8 otherwise), and keeps at most 40 questions on the whole board (those with the most evidence). First press Preview to see the list. Apply saves the old board first, so Undo can bring it back.
